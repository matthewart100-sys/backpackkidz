Why this night matters

The Life of Riley is a celebration first — an evening for the people whose lives Mike Riley has touched to be in the same room. Friends, family, former students, athletes, colleagues, and fans are all invited.

It is also a fundraiser. Every dollar raised goes to Back Pack Kidz, the volunteer-run program that packs weekend food bags for Charlotte County children who count on school meals during the week. When school closes on Friday, those bags are what carry a child through to Monday.

Come for the music and the memories. Leave knowing local kids will eat this weekend.

What your ticket does

$8.08 provides one weekend food bag.

$275 feeds one child across 34 school-week deliveries.

Can't make it on August 7th? A donation does the same work.
