GivingTuesday · December 1, 2026

Fill a backpack for the weekend.

When Charlotte County schools close on Friday, your gift helps our volunteers send children home with food for the weekend.

Donate with Zeffy Give monthly ↓

For a single gift, select One-time on the donation form.

Give $10 or $15 a month

A monthly gift helps us plan what to buy for upcoming packing days. On Zeffy, choose Monthly and select $10. For $15, enter 15 under Other amount.

Your gift repeats each month until you cancel. You can manage it through your Zeffy confirmation email.

Set up a monthly gift

Zeffy may suggest an optional contribution to its platform. To give only your chosen amount to Back Pack Kidz, select Other for the platform contribution and enter $0.
